Extension name:
HoverView — הגדלת תמונות

Suggested English headline (use in the description if the dashboard does not offer an English title):
HoverView — Bigger images on hover

Short description:
Preview larger images on hover, zoom, pin and rotate. Try higher-resolution sources when available. Hebrew interface.

Suggested category:
Tools, or the closest equivalent offered by the current dashboard.

Full description:
HoverView shows a larger image when you move your pointer over a picture, while keeping you on the page you are browsing.

When a website exposes a suitable higher-resolution source, HoverView tries to use it rather than simply enlarging the thumbnail. This includes original-image candidates for AliExpress images hosted on alicdn.com and aliexpress-media.com. Version 1.0.2 improves image loading and keeps stationary previews stable during unrelated page updates.

Features
• Preview images beside the pointer or in the center of the screen.
• Zoom using the mouse wheel or the + and - keys.
• Press Space to pin an image, then drag to pan after zooming.
• Rotate images and open their source in a separate tab.
• Choose a dark or light viewer, hover delay and maximum size.
• Require Alt to be held before showing a preview.
• Disable previews for an individual website or pause the extension globally.

The settings and viewer interface are in Hebrew. An included offline demo lets you try the controls. This is not an English translation of the interface; the English text describes the Hebrew-interface product.

Quick controls
Space: pin or close a pinned image.
Escape: close the viewer.
0: reset zoom and rotation.
R: rotate using an English keyboard layout.
O: open the image source in a new tab using an English keyboard layout.
Alt+Shift+H: enable or pause globally, if Chrome assigns the shortcut.

Privacy
No account, analytics or extension-operated processing server. Image URLs and captions are processed in the browser to render previews. Settings and per-site preferences are stored locally. Images are requested directly from their original image servers under the browser's normal policies.

Limitations
Compatibility varies by website. Chrome prevents extensions from running on internal browser pages and the Chrome Web Store. Canvas images, custom gallery implementations and protected image sources may not work. A small source image cannot gain details that do not exist in the source; HoverView does not provide AI upscaling.
